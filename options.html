Google Gemini API Key

MetaFrasi connects directly to Google. There is no MetaFrasi server between you and your translation.

New here? Get your API key in three steps
  1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in to your Google account.
  2. Create an API key for a Google project. To use the free tier, choose an eligible project without paid billing. Check availability and limits in AI Studio.
  3. Copy the key, paste it below, and choose Connect & save key.

Your own paid key also works. Google determines availability, quotas and charges. MetaFrasi does not enable billing or switch models automatically.

Your key stays in this browser profile. It is not synced.

Free-tier access is subject to Google's terms and usage limits. Model discovery does not verify pricing or generation quota. Pricing · Google API terms

Privacy & Data

No account with MetaFrasi, no analytics, no ads. Only the passage you explicitly submit is sent to Google, together with the translation instructions and your API key.

Recent results are cached temporarily in memory to avoid repeated requests. They are not stored as a reading history. Your API key is stored locally and is not encrypted by MetaFrasi.
